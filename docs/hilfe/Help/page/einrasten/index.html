Snapping

While drawing and moving, the pointer snaps to help points so everything sits exactly.

CncCraft has five kinds of snap point:

  • Node – corner points of existing shapes,
  • Centre – centre points,
  • Midpoint – the middle of an edge,
  • Job point – the corners and the centre of the workpiece,
  • Grid – the grid intersections.

A small hint beside the pointer names what it is snapping to.

Settings

The switches Snap to grid and Snap to geometry in the header (“Decoration” group) turn the kinds on and off. In View ▸ Snapping there is also:

  • To geometry (nodes, centres) and To job corners and centre separately,
  • Radius – how close in screen points the pointer must be,
  • To grid with the grid spacing in millimetres.

Hold ⇧ while dragging and nothing snaps.

The grid is only visible while Snap to grid is on – what you see is what snaps. In addition, moved shapes snap to construction lines: with an edge or their centre, and the line colours the shape red while it would snap.
