Grouping

Combine several shapes into a group that selects like a single shape.

Group (⌘G) combines the selection: a click on any part then selects the whole group. Groups can themselves be grouped.

Ungroup (⇧⌘G) dissolves the outermost group of the selection, one level per click. Copies of a group form their own new group (Copy, paste and duplicate).
