New compared with the original

These features CncCraft adds to what the Spark template can do:

  • The 3D view – The 3D view shows the workpiece as it will look after milling.
  • Keyboard shortcuts – The most important commands have keys. The “Keyboard shortcuts” list in the Help menu shows them in the program.
  • Saving and opening files – Projects are saved as <code>.cnccraft</code> files and hold the drawing, layers, variables, constraints and toolpaths.
  • Copy, paste and duplicate – Shapes can be copied and pasted – with their toolpaths and the constraints between them.
  • Draw a spline – A smooth curve through the points you click.
  • Pattern (array) – Turn one shape into a rectangular pattern of linked copies that stays alive with the shape.
  • Image as a template – Place a photo or sketch under the drawing, bring it to the right scale and trace it.
  • Parametric design – overview – Parametric CAD for beginners: dimensions need not be fixed – you name, compute and connect them, and the drawing follows.
  • Variables – Variables are named numbers or formulas you can use in every dimension field.
  • Formulas in dimension fields – Every number field does the maths: arithmetic, brackets, powers and variables.
  • Dimension distances – A distance constraint keeps two things a fixed distance apart – even when you move one or change variables.
  • Set level and upright – With one click a line stays level or upright – even when you drag the nodes.
  • Construction lines on the ruler – Red construction lines across the workpiece that shapes snap to and hang on – with a value, a formula and a lock.
  • The toolpath list – The list shows all toolpaths with colour, kind, name and estimated milling time.
  • Saving toolpaths – For the router you save the toolpaths as a G-code file.
  • Slot toolpath – The slot mills a channel along a vector, as wide as the cutter.
  • Materials and limits – Every workpiece is made of a material. It knows its limits, recommends cutters and cooling and sets the look in 3D.
  • Scripts and automation – A small script drives CncCraft step by step – the visible mouse shows every click. Great for short videos for TikTok, Instagram and the like.
